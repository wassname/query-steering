**Brainstormed Methods**

**1. Value Steering (v-steer)**
Instead of changing *what* the model looks at (queries), change *what it writes* when it looks at a token (values).
*   **Extract:** `v*_L = mean_i( v_L[pos_i, last] - v_L[neg_i, last] )`
*   **Apply:** `v_L[t] += α * v*_L` for all t
*   **Why it works:** Bypasses the complex query-key dot product dynamics. It injects the "honest" concept directly into the output of the attention heads regardless of where the model attends.
*   **Failure mode:** May cause repetition or overwrite distinct factual knowledge since it blindly adds to all values.
*   **Cost:** Low (1 forward pass to extract, standard generation overhead).

**2. Key Steering (k-steer)**
Make the "honest" context globally more attractive to queries.
*   **Extract:** `k*_L = mean_i( k_L[pos_i, instruction_tokens] - k_L[neg_i, instruction_tokens] )`
*   **Apply:** `k_L[t] += α * k*_L`
*   **Why it works:** If sycophancy stems from the user's prompt hijacking attention, boosting the key vectors of the system/honest instructions makes them more "retrievable."
*   **Failure mode:** Could break RoPE dynamics if the added vector interferes with positional encodings.
*   **Cost:** Low.

**3. Head-Masked Dom (q-mask-steer)**
Apply dom only to heads that actually differentiate honest/sycophantic behavior.
*   **Extract:** Calculate `q*_L_h` per head. Keep only heads where `std(pos_i - neg_i)` is lowest (most consistent direction).
*   **Apply:** `q_L_h[t] += α * q*_L_h` (only for top K heads).
*   **Why it works:** Dense softmax models (Qwen3) might suffer when dom shifts queries in heads dedicated to grammar or local context. Masking isolates semantic heads.
*   **Failure mode:** Selecting the wrong heads causes catastrophic degradation.
*   **Cost:** Low to extract, slightly higher engineering cost to apply per-head masks.

**4. Joint Residual & Attention Steering (Res-Q)**
*   **Extract:** Extract both `q*` (dom) and `h*` (residual mean-diff).
*   **Apply:** `q_L[t] += α * q*` AND `h_L[t] += β * h*` (with smaller α, β).
*   **Why it works:** Enhances the retrieval of honest facts (attention) while simultaneously shifting the output probability space (residual). 
*   **Failure mode:** Over-steering leading to rapid coherence loss (high damage).
*   **Cost:** Low.
